# Privacy Policy

**Effective as of Nov 2, 2026**

This privacy policy (the "Policy") explains the privacy practices of the developer ("we", "us", or "our") concerning the ikalendar2 application (the "App") for iPhone, iPad, Mac and any other devices or platforms.

## 1. Information We Collect

We do not collect, receive, or share any personal or non-personal data through the App. The App has no accounts, analytics, advertising, or tracking.

## 2. Network Requests

The App connects directly to the servers below. As with any internet request, the operator of each server can see your device's IP address and basic request details; the App sends no identifiers or personal data with these requests.

- splatoon2.ink: to download public Splatoon 2 schedule data.
- api.github.com: when you open an open-source license in Settings, to download its text.
- www.google.com and www.facebook.com: when you open the alternate app icon screen, to check whether internet access is restricted in your region.

These services are governed by their own privacy policies. Links you tap open in your browser or the App Store.

## 3. Data Stored on Your Device

Your settings are stored only on your device and are deleted when you delete the App. Motion-sensor readings used for visual effects are processed on the device and are never stored or transmitted.

## 4. Data Retention and Deletion

Because we do not collect any data, there is nothing for us to retain or delete.

## 5. Your Acceptance of The Policy

Your continued use of the App will be deemed your acceptance of the Policy.

## 6. Changes to The Policy

We reserve the right to change or modify any of the terms and conditions contained in the Policy, in its sole discretion, by posting any changes at this current page (https://ikalendar.app/privacy-policy). Your continued use of any part of the App following the posting of such changes or modifications will constitute your acceptance of such changes or modifications.
